

## Definition

Psychosocial risks arise from deficiencies in work design, organization, and management, as well as from poor social context at work. They include excessive demands, low control, poor support, lack of recognition, isolation, work-family conflict, and workplace violence or harassment. They affect mental health and well-being.

## Main characteristics

- Excessive workload and time pressure
    
- Low autonomy and decision latitude
    
- Poor social support from supervisors and colleagues
    
- Lack of recognition and reward
    
- Job insecurity
    
- Work-family conflict
    
- Social isolation and loneliness
    
- Workplace violence and harassment
    
- Technostress and digital overload
