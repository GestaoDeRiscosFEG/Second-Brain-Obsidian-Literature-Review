## Definition

Cognitive risks arise from excessive mental workload, information overload, attention demands, complex decision-making, and cognitive fatigue. They are particularly relevant in knowledge work, remote work, and digital environments where workers face constant connectivity and multitasking.

## Main characteristics

- Mental overload
    
- Information overload
    
- Attention fragmentation
    
- Multitasking demands
    
- Complex decision-making under pressure
    
- Cognitive fatigue
    
- Concentration difficulties
    
- Technostress
    
- Digital fatigue
