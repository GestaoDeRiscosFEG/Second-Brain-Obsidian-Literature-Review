## Definition

Physical risks are hazards arising from physical agents present in the work environment that can cause harm to workers. They include noise, extreme temperatures, inadequate lighting, vibration, radiation, and biological agents. In the context of remote and hybrid work, they also include inadequate home environment conditions and domestic accidents.

## Main characteristics

- Excessive noise
    
- Inadequate lighting
    
- Extreme temperatures
    
- Vibration
    
- Radiation
    
- Biological agents
    
- Domestic accidents
    
- Inadequate ventilation and air quality
