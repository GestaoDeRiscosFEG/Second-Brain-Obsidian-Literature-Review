## Definition

Organizational risks arise from how work is designed, organized, and managed. They include excessive working hours, shift work, job insecurity, unclear roles, poor leadership, inadequate communication, lack of training, and organizational culture issues. They affect both worker health and organizational performance.

## Main characteristics

- Long working hours
    
- Shift work and irregular schedules
    
- Job insecurity
    
- Unclear roles and responsibilities
    
- Poor leadership and communication
    
- Lack of training and development
    
- Organizational change without participation
    
- Inadequate policies
    
- Unfair reward systems
