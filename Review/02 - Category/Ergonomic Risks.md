
## Definition

Ergonomic risks arise from inadequate workstation setup, poor posture, repetitive movements, excessive physical effort, inappropriate furniture, or work organization that affects the worker's body. They include MSDs (Musculoskeletal Disorders), RSI (Repetitive Strain Injuries), low back pain, neck pain, and upper limb pain.

## Main characteristics

- Inadequate and prolonged static postures
    
- Repetitive movements
    
- Manual material handling and lifting
    
- Inadequate furniture and equipment
    
- Poor lighting, temperature, and noise
    
- Work organization that promotes sedentary behavior
