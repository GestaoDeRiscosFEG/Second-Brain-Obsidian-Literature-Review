### Paluszak et al. (2025)

#### Reference (ABNT)
PALUSZAK, Adam, et al. Shaping well-being and healthy habits at work through tailored physical activity among remote and hybrid employees: study protocol for RCT. **BMJ Open**, v. 15, art. e094595, p. 1-11, 2025. DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2024-094595.

#### Objective
To propose and validate a comprehensive interdisciplinary intervention model designed to shape sustainable well-being and healthy work habits through tailored physical activity among remote and hybrid employees spending at least half of their workday in a seated position, comparing tailored training against general physical activity and a no-intervention control group.

#### Research questions or hypotheses
Is tailored physical activity training superior to general exercise in improving workplace well-being and reducing musculoskeletal discomfort among sedentary teleworkers? Does tailored training incorporating neuromuscular education foster sustainable exercise habits three months post-intervention? Does tailored training yield greater long-term reductions in virtual sickness presenteeism compared to general exercise or no intervention?

#### Method
*   **Type:** Study protocol for a three-arm randomized controlled trial (RCT) combining subjective psychometric questionnaires and objective biomechanical laboratory measurements
*   **Application:** Target cohort of 90 remote or hybrid employees (aged 20 to 65 years) across administration, finance, and IT sectors in Poland, randomized into tailored exercise (12 supervised physiotherapist sessions with muscle mechanism explanations), general exercise, or control, assessed at baseline, post-intervention (week 4), and follow-up (month 3)
*   **Type of work:** Remote and hybrid office work

#### Main findings
As a published trial protocol, the article outlines the theoretical framework and anticipated outcomes. The authors hypothesize that while general physical activity yields transient, short-term health relief, a tailored physical activity regimen specifically targeting postural muscles strained by prolonged sitting (erector spinae, trapezius, cervical flexors)—coupled with explicit cognitive explanations of neuromuscular biomechanics—will promote autonomous habit formation and sustained long-term health improvements. Expected outcomes include marked reductions in localized musculoskeletal pain, significant improvements in muscle elasticity and mechanical stiffness (measured via myotonometry), decreased muscle fatigue during typing tasks (measured via surface electromyography), elevated holistic worker well-being (NIOSH WellBQ), and substantial decreases in virtual sickness presenteeism.

#### Identified risks
*   **Classification:** Ergonomic, Psychosocial, Physical
*   **Specific risk factors:** Cumulative occupational sedentary time (spending 77% to 82% of workdays seated), unergonomic domestic setups, lack of structured movement routines, internal performance pressure, blurred work-life boundaries, emotional exhaustion, and virtual presenteeism (working while ill due to lack of visibility)
*   **Impacts and consequences:** Work-related musculoskeletal disorders (cervical, thoracic, and lumbar strain), permanent neuromuscular dysfunction, impaired daytime alertness, chronic psychological stress, and loss of productive organizational time

#### Risk management strategies
Designing and delivering tailored physical activity programs supervised by qualified physiotherapists targeting deep postural muscles. Integrating educational modules explaining the biomechanical rationale for each movement to foster long-term autonomous habit adherence. Establishing organizational cultures that discourage virtual presenteeism and promote active rest breaks. Deploying combined objective biomechanical diagnostic tools (EMG and myotonometry) alongside subjective self-reports to track worker physiological fatigue and intervention effectiveness.

#### Tools and techniques
- [[Surface Electromyography (EMG)]]: Objective physiological measurement of muscle fatigue and electrical activity in the upper trapezius and lumbar erector spinae during standardized computer typing tests.
- [[Myotonometry (MyotonPRO)]]: Non-invasive digital biomechanical assessment determining muscle stiffness, elasticity, and tone in resting and contracted states.
- [[Nordic Musculoskeletal Questionnaire (NMQ)]]: Diagnostic questionnaire evaluating the 12-month and 7-day prevalence and anatomical location of musculoskeletal complaints.
- [[NIOSH WellBQ]]: 63-item comprehensive instrument developed by NIOSH measuring five holistic domains of worker well-being.
- [[Stanford Presenteeism Scale (SPS-6)]]: 6-item validated psychometric instrument assessing self-rated health status and productivity impairment.
- [[Visual Analogue Scale (VAS)]]: 100 mm scale quantifying subjective pain intensity across distinct anatomical regions.

#### Relation to regulations
The protocol references recent statutory amendments to the Polish Labor Code (Journal of Laws 2025, item 277) mandating that employers ensure safe and healthy working conditions in remote work, but observes that statutory enforcement remains superficial, relying largely on employee self-declarations without objective verification. The trial received ethical approval from the Rector's Commission on Research Ethics at Wroclaw University of Economics and Business (Decision 10/2025).

#### Gaps pointed out by the authors
Generic corporate wellness and fitness initiatives frequently fail to provide sustained benefits because they do not account for the specific biomechanical demands of home-based computer work or integrate habit-formation behavioral components. Furthermore, very few workplace studies combine subjective questionnaire surveys with objective physiological and biomechanical diagnostic tools (such as EMG and myotonometry). Methodological limitations noted include potential participant reporting bias, uncontrolled home environmental factors, and the challenge of sustaining long-term exercise compliance without continuous supervision.

#### Other relevant information
The trial incorporates explicit educational scaffolding on neuromuscular mechanisms to bridge the gap between declared health knowledge and real-world behavioral compliance in remote work.
