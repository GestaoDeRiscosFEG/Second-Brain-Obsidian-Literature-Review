# WRRQ

## Description

Questionnaire to assess work-related rumination, including affective rumination, psychological detachment from work, and problem-solving pondering. Used to evaluate recovery after work.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Affective rumination
    
- Psychological detachment
    
- Problem-solving pondering
    

## How it is applied

Self-administered questionnaire. Number of items not reported in the extraction. Can be applied in person or online.

**Limitations:** Number of items not reported; may overlap with other recovery measures.
