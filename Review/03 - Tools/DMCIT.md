# DMCIT

## Description

Scientific questionnaire of 9 questions developed to diagnose, guide, and train healthy leadership behaviors in remote and hybrid work. Assesses and prevents psychosocial risks in the virtual environment, divided into two behavioral dimensions: Supportive ICT-Mediated Interaction (SIMI) and Avoidance of Abusive ICT Adoption (AAIA).

## Origin and development

Developed by Cioffi et al. (2025) in the context of Italian public administration. Based on the literature on management competencies, occupational stress, and psychosocial risks in digital work. Validated in a sample of more than 200 workers and more than 50 managers.

## What it measures

- Supportive ICT-Mediated Interaction (SIMI, 5 items): measures the manager's ability to communicate clearly and organized through virtual means, give agile feedback at a distance, choose the appropriate channel for each conversation, and provide support in emergencies
    
- Avoidance of Abusive ICT Adoption (AAIA, 4 items): assesses whether the manager respects the employee's personal boundaries, avoiding work demands outside working hours (nights, days off, vacations, or sick leave) and refraining from invasive digital monitoring or micro-management (over-monitoring)
    

## How it is applied

9-item questionnaire, answered by remote or hybrid workers about their manager's behavior. Can be used as an organizational health diagnosis, manager self-reflection exercises, upward feedback processes, and to design internal right-to-disconnect policies.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Specific to the digital context, brief, allows upward feedback, guides practical leadership interventions.

**Limitations:** Cross-sectional study; limited comparative base; does not comprehensively assess all digital management competencies; predominantly hybrid sample.
