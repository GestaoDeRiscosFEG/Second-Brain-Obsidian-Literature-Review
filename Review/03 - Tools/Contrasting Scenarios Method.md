# Contrasting Scenarios Method

## Description

Qualitative method from INRS that generates plausible and opposite narrative frameworks about the future ("Each worker as autonomous provider", "Heterogeneous organizations", "Flexicurity", and "Companies in survival mode") to identify new emerging OSH risks in advance.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Plausible future scenarios
    
- Emerging occupational risks
    
- Organizational changes and their impacts
    

## How it is applied

Qualitative method that builds contrasting scenarios based on uncertainty variables and expert analysis. Used to anticipate risks before they materialize.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Héry et al. (2022)]]: Used to build contrasting scenarios about the future of work and identify emerging OSH risks.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Allows anticipation of emerging risks, stimulates strategic reflection, involves experts.

**Limitations:** Requires expertise; scenarios are plausible but not predictive; depends on the quality of the variables chosen.
