# SHARE Model

## Description

Model proposed by Cheung (2024) for preliminary risk assessment and wellbeing management in work-from-home. Emphasizes the employer's responsibility to assess whether the home workstation meets ergonomic and safety requirements, providing equipment and support when necessary.

## Origin and development

Proposed by Victor K. L. Cheung (2024) in the context of post-pandemic wellbeing management in remote work. Based on literature on ergonomics, occupational health, and wellbeing management.

## What it measures

- Preliminary assessment of the home workstation
    
- Compliance with ergonomic requirements
    
- Need for supplementary equipment
    
- Operational, technical, and technological support provided by the employer
    
- General team wellbeing
    

## How it is applied

Used as a framework for preliminary risk assessment in home office. The employer assesses whether the environment is adequate; if not, must provide equipment or alternatives (e.g., rented office, conference room).

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Specific to the home office context, emphasizes employer responsibility, guides practical actions.

**Limitations:** Developed-in-study tool without identified external validation; scope limited to the physical environment; does not capture complex psychosocial risks.
