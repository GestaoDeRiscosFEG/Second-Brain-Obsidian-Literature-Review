# WEMS

## Description

Self-assessment questionnaire of psychosocial work experience, developed from a salutogenic perspective (focused on health-promoting resources rather than disease). Assesses six dimensions of the work environment.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Supportive working conditions: support among colleagues, organizational atmosphere
    
- Inner work experience: meaning of work, personal challenges
    
- Autonomy: decision-making power over pace and tasks
    
- Time experience: ability to complete work within regular hours without pressure or stress
    
- Leadership: availability and support from the direct manager
    
- Change process: open dialogue and active participation in organizational restructuring
    

## How it is applied

Questionnaire with 32 items on a 6-point agreement scale (from "strongly disagree" to "strongly agree"). Scores are standardized from 0 to 100. Can be applied before and after interventions or organizational changes.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Salutogenic perspective, covers multiple dimensions, allows longitudinal analysis, validated.

**Limitations:** Originally developed for on-site contexts; requires adaptation for remote and hybrid work; does not capture risks specific to the digital environment.
