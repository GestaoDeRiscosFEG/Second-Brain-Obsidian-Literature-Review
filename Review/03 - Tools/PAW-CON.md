# PAW-CON

## Description

Construction-specific psychosocial risk assessment survey based on the JD-R (Job Demands-Resources) model. Assesses job demands and job resources relevant to the construction industry.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Job demands
    
- Job resources
    
- Psychosocial risk factors
    

## How it is applied

Survey with 36 items. Can be applied in person or online.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Biggs et al. (2025)]]: Used to assess job demands and resources in construction workers before and after a holistic psychosocial intervention.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Construction-specific, based on JD-R model, covers key psychosocial dimensions.

**Limitations:** Specific to construction industry; may not be applicable to other sectors.
