# SCS-SF

## Description

Self-assessment scale of self-compassion, defined as the ability to treat oneself with kindness in moments of difficulty, recognize the common humanity of experiences, and maintain mindfulness. The short form has 12 items.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Self-kindness: treating oneself with kindness
    
- Common humanity: recognizing that difficulties are shared
    
- Mindfulness: present-moment awareness
    
- Self-judgment (reverse): excessive self-criticism
    
- Isolation (reverse): feeling alone in difficulties
    
- Over-identification (reverse): fusion with problems
    

## How it is applied

5-point Likert scale (1 = almost never to 5 = almost always). The short version has 12 items and takes about 3 to 5 minutes. Can be applied in person or online.

- [[Andel et al. (2021)]]: Used as a moderator of the relationship between work loneliness and depression.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Brief, validated, captures relevant psychological resource, allows moderation assessment.

**Limitations:** Does not replace clinical assessment; may overlap with other constructs (e.g., resilience, self-esteem); does not capture all aspects of self-compassion.
