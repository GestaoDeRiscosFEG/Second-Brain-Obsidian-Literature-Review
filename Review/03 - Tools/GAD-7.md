# GAD-7

## Description

Brief screening and severity assessment instrument for generalized anxiety symptoms, based on DSM-IV diagnostic criteria. One of the most used instruments in research and clinical practice for anxiety.

## Origin and development

Developed by Spitzer et al. (2006). Validated in several populations and contexts. Widely used in occupational health and mental health research.

## What it measures

- 7 items corresponding to generalized anxiety symptoms
    
- Frequency of symptoms in the last 2 weeks (scale 0 to 3)
    
- Total score from 0 to 21 (severity: minimal, mild, moderate, severe)
    
- Allows screening and longitudinal follow-up
    

## How it is applied

Self-administered questionnaire with 7 items on a 4-point Likert scale (0 = never to 3 = nearly every day). Can be applied in person or online.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Brief, low cost, validated, sensitive for screening, allows longitudinal follow-up.

**Limitations:** Does not replace clinical diagnosis; may overestimate or underestimate prevalence; does not capture all aspects of anxiety.
