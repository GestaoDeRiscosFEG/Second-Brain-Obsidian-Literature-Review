# WSM

## Description

Analytical framework for mapping and understanding work systems. Identifies 9 interrelated elements that compose a work system and allows analysis of how changes in one element affect the others.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Participants: people who perform the work
    
- Information: data and knowledge used
    
- Technologies: tools and systems
    
- Work practices: procedures and routines
    
- Products/Services: results of work
    
- Customers: recipients of products or services
    
- Environment: physical and organizational context
    
- Infrastructure: support resources
    
- Strategies: objectives and guidelines
    

## How it is applied

Used as an analytical framework to map work systems and identify impacts of changes (e.g., transition to remote work). Can be combined with qualitative methods (interviews, observation).

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Comprehensive, allows systemic analysis, identifies interrelations, applicable to various contexts.

**Limitations:** Not a risk assessment method per se; requires adaptation to specific contexts; may be complex for SMEs with limited resources.

## Applicability to SMEs and remote or hybrid work
