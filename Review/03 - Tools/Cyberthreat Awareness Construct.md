# Cyberthreat Awareness Construct

## Description

Scale of awareness about cyber threats in the home office context. Part of the study by Klein and Zwilling (2024) on employee cyber-defense behaviors while working from home.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction. The study by Klein and Zwilling (2024) investigated the antecedents of active cyber-defense behaviors and cyber-risk behaviors among employees working from home.

## What it measures
