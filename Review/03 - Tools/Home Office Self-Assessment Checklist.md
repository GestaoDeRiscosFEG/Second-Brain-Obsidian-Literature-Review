# Home Office Self-Assessment Checklist

## Description

Structured self-assessment tool filled in by the teleworker, containing objective questions about space, ventilation, lighting, and ergonomic arrangement of the home workstation. Available on the Latvian national portal [www.stradavesels.lv](https://www.stradavesels.lv/).

## Origin and development

Developed by Latvian public authorities (Ministry of Welfare / Institute of Occupational Safety and Environmental Health of Riga Stradins University). Used by Matisāne et al. (2021) during the first wave of the COVID-19 pandemic.

## What it measures

- Space available for work
    
- Ventilation and air quality
    
- Lighting
    
- Ergonomic arrangement (desk, chair, monitor, keyboard)
    
- Noise
    
- General workstation conditions
    

## How it is applied

Checklist filled in by the worker. Can be sent by email or made available online. Employers can request complementary photos for technical analysis by OSH specialists.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Matisāne et al. (2021)]]: Used as a viable and effective method to identify risks in the home office without in-person visits.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Low cost, easy to apply, allows remote screening, empowers the worker.

**Limitations:** Self-report; may have social desirability bias; does not replace on-site technical assessment.

## Applicability to SMEs and remote or hybrid work
