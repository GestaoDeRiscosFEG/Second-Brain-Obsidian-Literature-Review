# Morphological Analysis

## Description

Systematic foresight technique used to map all possible combinations of uncertainty variables. In the study by Héry et al. (2022), the authors defined 6 organizational variables (role of telework, work collectives, labor relations, automation, social utility, and training) with competing hypotheses to build 4 global scenarios about the future of work up to 2025.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Uncertainty variables
    
- Possible combinations
    
- Scenarios
    

## How it is applied

Systematic mapping of variables and their competing hypotheses, generating a matrix of possible combinations that are then analyzed to build scenarios.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Systematic, allows exploration of multiple futures, identifies emerging risks.

**Limitations:** Requires expertise; results depend on the variables and hypotheses chosen.
