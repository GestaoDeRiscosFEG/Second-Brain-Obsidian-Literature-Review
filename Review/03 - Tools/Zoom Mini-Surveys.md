# Zoom Mini-Surveys

## Description

Functionality of the Zoom platform that allows creating short and anonymous surveys during online sessions, used for collecting quantitative data and triangulation with qualitative data.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Quick and anonymous responses
    
- Data triangulation
    
- Quantitative perceptions
    

## How it is applied

Created during Zoom sessions, with multiple-choice or scale questions. Five-question mini-surveys were used in the study by Chen et al. (2024).

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Chen et al. (2024)]]: Used for quantitative triangulation during focus groups with OSH professionals in Ireland.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Fast, anonymous, easy to use, integrated into Zoom.

**Limitations:** Limited to online sessions; does not replace validated questionnaires.
