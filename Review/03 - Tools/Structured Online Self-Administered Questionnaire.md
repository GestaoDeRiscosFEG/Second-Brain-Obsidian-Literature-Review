# Structured Online Self-Administered Questionnaire

## Description

Online quantitative instrument composed of 4 sections: (1) demographic data, (2) home work characteristics, (3) physical and psychosocial health effects (differentiating new onset from worsening), and (4) lifestyle changes. Content validity was assessed by 5 specialists in occupational health, occupational medicine, and psychology (IOC between 0.8 and 1.0), with test-retest reliability between 90% and 100%.

## Origin and development

Developed by Ekpanyaskul and Padungtod (2021) for the study on occupational health problems and lifestyle changes among novice working-from-home workers during the COVID-19 pandemic in Thailand.

## What it measures

- Demographic data
    
- Home work characteristics
    
- Physical and psychosocial health effects
    
- Lifestyle changes
    

## How it is applied

Online structured questionnaire with 4 sections. Content validity assessed by specialists. Test-retest reliability reported as 90% to 100%.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Content validity assessed by specialists, test-retest reliability reported, covers multiple dimensions.

**Limitations:** Self-report; developed for a specific context (Thailand, early pandemic); may require adaptation for other populations.
