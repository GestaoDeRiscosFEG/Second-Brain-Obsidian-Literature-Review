# Workstation Score Assessment

## Description

Categorical assessment tool (-1, 0, +1) calculated from a list of furniture and equipment elements. Defines baseline level (0) as the presence of adjustable chair, monitor, keyboard, and mouse; deficient level (-1) as the exclusive use of laptop or fixed dining chair; and enhanced level (+1) for the presence of height-adjustable desk or multiple monitors.

## Origin and development

Developed by McAllister et al. (2022) for the study on teleworker discomfort during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## What it measures

- Presence of adjustable chair
    
- Presence of monitor
    
- Presence of keyboard and mouse
    
- Presence of height-adjustable desk
    
- Presence of multiple monitors
    

## How it is applied

Categorical scoring (-1, 0, +1) based on a checklist of furniture and equipment. Applied by the researcher or self-reported by the worker.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[McAllister et al. (2022)]]: Used to classify home office workstations and analyze their association with musculoskeletal discomfort.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Simple, quick, allows categorization of workstations.

**Limitations:** Does not assess how the workstation adjusts to the worker; limited to furniture and equipment.
