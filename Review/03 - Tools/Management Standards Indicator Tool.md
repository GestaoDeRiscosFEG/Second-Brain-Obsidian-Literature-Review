# Management Standards Indicator Tool

## Description

Validated questionnaire adapted from the UK HSE framework to assess supervisor support, role, demands, and control. Part of the HSE Management Standards for work-related stress.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Demands
    
- Control
    
- Manager support
    
- Peer support
    
- Relationships
    
- Role
    
- Change
    

## How it is applied

Self-administered questionnaire. Number of items not reported in the extraction. Can be applied in person or online.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Cioffi et al. (2025)]]: Used to assess supervisor support, role, demands, and control as part of the concurrent validity analysis of the DMCIT.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Validated, based on the HSE Management Standards, covers key psychosocial dimensions.

**Limitations:** Developed for the UK context; may require adaptation to other cultures; number of items not reported.
