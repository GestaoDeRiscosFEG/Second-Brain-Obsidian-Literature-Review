# Material Teleworking Conditions Checklist

## Description

Four-item binary checklist (Yes/No) to assess the material conditions of telework: presence of a dedicated room or space for home work, use of a second computer monitor, use of an office chair, and use of a dedicated keyboard.

## Origin and development

Developed by Fouquet et al. (2025) for the study on low back pain and teleworking conditions during lockdown in France.

## What it measures

- Presence of a dedicated room or space for home work
    
- Use of a second computer monitor
    
- Use of an office chair
    
- Use of a dedicated keyboard
    

## How it is applied

Four binary items (Yes/No). Quick application, can be self-administered.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Fouquet et al. (2025)]]: Used to assess material teleworking conditions and their association with low back pain onset.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Very brief, easy to apply, covers key material dimensions.

**Limitations:** Does not assess how equipment is used; limited to material conditions.
