# SBQ-R

## Description

Brief instrument to assess suicidal thoughts and behaviors, including history of suicide attempts and risk of future suicidal behavior. Used in occupational health research to screen for suicide risk.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Suicidal ideation
    
- History of suicide attempts
    
- Risk of future suicidal behavior
    
- Communication of suicidal intent
    

## How it is applied

Self-administered questionnaire. Number of items not reported in the extraction. Takes a few minutes.

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Brief, validated, specific for suicide risk.

**Limitations:** Sensitive topic; requires ethical protocols; does not replace clinical assessment.
