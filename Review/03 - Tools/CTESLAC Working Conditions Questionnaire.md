# CTESLAC

## Description

Standardized occupational health survey instrument recommended to incorporate specific ILO questions about the home workplace and the use of ICTs, allowing monitoring and comparison of the health impacts of telework between Latin American and Caribbean countries.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Working conditions
    
- Employment
    
- Health
    
- ICT use
    
- Home workplace
    
- Health impacts of telework
    

## How it is applied

Standardized survey applied at the population level. Can be adapted for different countries. Allows international comparison.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Benavides et al. (2021)]]: Recommended to monitor the health impacts of telework in Latin America and the Caribbean.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Standardized, allows international comparison, includes specific questions on telework, based on ILO recommendations.

**Limitations:** Requires adaptation for each country; may not capture local specificities; depends on research infrastructure.
