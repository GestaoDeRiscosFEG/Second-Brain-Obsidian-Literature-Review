# Discomfort Body Map

## Description

Structured online self-report survey with a body map of 23 anatomical regions, analytically compiled into 3 main regions (upper limb/arm, neck, and back). Assesses the presence, onset (new discomfort), and worsening (clinically relevant change of ±15 points) of discomfort before and after the migration to home office.

## Origin and development

Developed by McAllister et al. (2022) based on the Work Environment and Health (WEH) Survey (Robertson et al., 2009).

## What it measures

- Presence of discomfort in 23 anatomical regions
    
- Onset of new discomfort
    
- Worsening of pre-existing discomfort (change of ±15 points)
    
- General workstation comfort
    

## How it is applied

Online self-administered questionnaire. Can be applied before and after changes in the workstation. Takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[McAllister et al. (2022)]]: Used to assess discomfort before and after the transition to telework, with 23 anatomical regions compiled into 3 main regions.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Detailed, covers 23 anatomical regions, allows before/after comparison.

**Limitations:** Extensive; self-report; may have recall bias.
