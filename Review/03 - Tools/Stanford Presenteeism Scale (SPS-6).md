# SPS-6

## Description

Scale to assess presenteeism, defined as the phenomenon of working while sick or with health problems, resulting in reduced productivity. Assesses the impact of health problems on work performance.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Health-related presenteeism
    
- Impact on productivity
    
- Difficulty concentrating
    
- Ability to perform tasks
    
- Total score indicates level of presenteeism
    

## How it is applied

Likert scale (usually 5 points) with 6 items. Can be applied in person or online. Allows assessment of the impact of interventions on productivity.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Paluszak et al. (2025)]]: Used as a presenteeism measure in a randomized controlled trial protocol.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Brief, validated, captures the impact of health on productivity, allows longitudinal follow-up.

**Limitations:** Self-report; does not replace objective productivity assessment; may have social desirability bias.
