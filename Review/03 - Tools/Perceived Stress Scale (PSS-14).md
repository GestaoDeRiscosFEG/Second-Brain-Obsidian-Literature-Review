# PSS-14

## Description

Self-assessment scale of perceived stress, defined as the degree to which the individual perceives life events as unpredictable, uncontrollable, and overloaded. One of the most used scales for psychological stress assessment.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- General perceived stress
    
- Sense of unpredictability
    
- Sense of uncontrollability
    
- Sense of overload
    
- Total score indicates level of perceived stress
    

## How it is applied

5-point Likert scale (0 = never to 4 = very often). The 14-item version takes about 5 minutes. Can be applied in person or online.

- [[Rodríguez-Nogueira et al. (2021)]]: Spanish version used to assess perceived stress in university workers during the pandemic.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Validated, low cost, allows longitudinal follow-up, sensitive to change.

**Limitations:** Does not replace clinical assessment; may overlap with anxiety and depression; does not capture work-specific stressors.
