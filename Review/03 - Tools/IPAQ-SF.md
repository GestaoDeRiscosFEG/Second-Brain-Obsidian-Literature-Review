# IPAQ-SF

## Description

Standardized questionnaire for assessing physical activity in adult populations. The short form assesses frequency, intensity, and duration of physical activities in leisure, work, transport, and domestic domains, in addition to sitting time.

## Origin and development

Developed by the IPAQ Group (1998-2000), an international consortium of researchers. Validated in several countries and languages.

## What it measures

- Physical activity in leisure time
    
- Physical activity at work
    
- Physical activity in transport
    
- Domestic physical activity
    
- Sitting time
    
- Physical activity level (low, moderate, high)
    

## How it is applied

Self-administered questionnaire or interview. The short form has 7 items and takes about 5 minutes. Can be applied in person or online.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Paluszak et al. (2025)]]: Used as a measure of physical activity in a randomized controlled trial protocol.
    
- [[Rodríguez-Nogueira et al. (2021)]]: Used to quantify sitting hours and physical effort before and during lockdown.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Validated internationally, allows cross-country comparison, covers multiple domains, low cost.

**Limitations:** Self-report; may have recall bias; does not capture unstructured physical activity.
