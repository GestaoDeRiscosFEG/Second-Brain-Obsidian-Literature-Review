# Rest Break Frequency Scale

## Description

Single-item adapted scale to measure the frequency of rest breaks during the workday. The original 5-point scale was recoded into 3 dummy variables: Low (no breaks or lunch only), Medium (2-3 times per day), and High (4 or more times per day).

## Origin and development

Developed or adapted by Cropley et al. (2023) for the study on rest breaks and well-being during lockdown.

## What it measures

- Frequency of rest breaks during the workday
    
- Categorized into low, medium, or high frequency
    

## How it is applied

Single item on a 5-point scale, recoded into 3 categories. Takes less than 1 minute.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Cropley et al. (2023)]]: Used to assess the association between rest break frequency and musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, and sleep problems in remote workers.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Very brief, easy to apply, captures a key recovery behavior.

**Limitations:** Single item; does not capture break duration or quality; limited psychometric properties.
