# LSNS-R

## Description

Scale to assess the individual's social support network, including size, diversity, and frequency of contacts with family, friends, and neighbors. Allows identification of risk of social isolation.

## Origin and development

Not reported in the extraction.

## What it measures

- Social network size
    
- Diversity of contacts (family, friends, neighbors)
    
- Frequency of contacts
    
- Perceived social support
    
- Total score indicates level of social isolation (higher score means greater isolation)
    

## How it is applied

5-point scale. The revised version has 12 items (6 for family and 6 for friends). Takes about 5 minutes. Can be applied in person or online.

## How it was used in the studies

- [[Alroomi and Mohamed (2022)]]: Used to measure objective social isolation among isolated oil and gas workers in Kuwait.
    

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages:** Validated, covers multiple dimensions of the social network, allows identification of isolation risk.

**Limitations:** Self-report; does not capture quality of relationships; may have recall bias.
